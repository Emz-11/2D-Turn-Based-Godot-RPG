 !! IMPORTANT !!

GAME CONTROLS ARE AS FOLLOWS

  W, S, A, D > DIRECTIONAL MOVEMENT

  B  > TELEPORT PLAYER CHARACTER TO THE CAMP SITE
	WALKING BY THE FIRE WILL ALLOW USER TO LEVEL UP
	WALKING BY THE MONK WILL HEAL THE USER TO FULL ONCE THEY LEAVE CAMP

  V  > TELEPORTS PLAYER CHARACTER BACK TO THE WORLD MAP
  MOUSE CLICK > TO SELECT SKILLS OR PRESS BUTTONS


	THE FIRST FIGHT OF THE GAME WILL BE THE TOUGHEST BUT ALSO GRANT ENOUGH EXPERIENCE POINTS FOR MULTIPLE LEVELS, RECOMMENDED TO START EVERY FIGHT USING "SKILL"
	IT IS RECOMMENDED YOU GO BACK TO CAMP AFTER EACH FIGHT TO LEVEL UP AND HEAL, EACH LEVEL UP GRANTS PLAYER CHARACTER:
	+1 IN EVERY STAT AND 3 SKILL POINTS TO USE, EACH SKILL POINT USED WILL INCREASE A SPECIFIC STAT BY 3

	THE PLAYER CHARACTER CAN INVEST INTO ONE OF FOUR STATS:

	VIGOR: INCREASES PLAYER TOTAL HP AND THE AMOUNT OF HEALING RECEIVED FROM HEAL SPELL DURING COMBAT 
	STRENGTH: INCREASES PLAYER ATTACK AND SKILL DAMAGE BY A FLAT AMOUNT PROPORTIONATE TO TOTAL STRENGTH
	DEXTERITY: INCREASES PLAYER CRITICAL CHANCE AND CRITICAL DAMAGE
	ARMOR: DECREASES DAMAGE TAKEN FROM ENEMIES

	CURRENT STRONGEST STATS TO FULLY INVEST INTO ARE:
	
	1. ARMOR > STRONGEST BY FAR, CAN MAKE THE USER PRACTICALLY INVINCIBLE BY THE TIME THEY REACH THE BOSS FIGHT
	2. STRENGTH > MASSIVE INCREASE IN DAMAGE DONE, HARD TO LOSE
	3. VIGOR > MASSIVE INCREASE IN HEALTH TOTAL AND IN AMOUNT YOU CAN HEAL THROUGH THE HEAL SPELL, MORE OR LESS UNKILLABLE
	4. DEXTERITY > WEAKEST BY FAR, USER WILL LIKELY LOSE BOSSFIGHT IF THEY CAN'T HIT A BIG CRIT OR TWO
